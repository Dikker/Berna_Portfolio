My Family

This is my mom, and my brother. The people who watched me grow up, taught me discipline, whom the people I love so much, and the people who will always be there for me no matter what.

Scallywag (SLCV Dance Company)

I'm so glad that I met these peeps, especially my ate's and kuya's, but we all had to walk on our own paths. However, we were still able to cross our paths and hangout again whenever we had time. I love these people so much, I love performing and practicing with them, and I will forever cherish our memories together.

Church Choir

Serving the Lord through music and singing in our church choir brings so much peace and joy to my heart. I am glad that I met these people and I always enjoy singing in church with them, having breakfast together every after mass.
